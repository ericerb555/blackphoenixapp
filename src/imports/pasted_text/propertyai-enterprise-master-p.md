AI Vision & Document Intelligence

Allow users to upload photos, videos, PDFs, spreadsheets, inspection reports, invoices, reserve studies, governing documents, warranties, manuals, and maintenance records.

The AI should:

-   Organize files automatically.
-   Extract relevant information.
-   Build timelines of repairs and improvements.
-   Identify visible maintenance concerns from images.
-   Compare historical images.
-   Build searchable property histories.
-   Flag missing information for user review.

Users must be able to review, edit, or reject AI-extracted information.
