AI Property Revenue Intelligence Module

Create an advanced AI module called Property Revenue Intelligence for the landlord, commercial property, property manager, and condominium association portals.

Purpose

The purpose of this AI is to analyze all available information about a property and identify practical opportunities to:

-   Generate additional monthly or annual income
-   Reduce operating and maintenance expenses
-   Improve the use of underperforming property assets
-   Offset condo fees or association dues
-   Increase net operating income
-   Strengthen reserve funding
-   Improve long-term property value

The AI must provide suggestions based on the specific property rather than giving only generic recommendations.

AI Follow-Up Questions

When property information is incomplete, the AI should ask targeted questions such as:

-   How many unused parking spaces are available?
-   Is there currently a laundry room?
-   Are storage areas assigned to residents?
-   Is the roof leased or used for equipment?
-   Are association documents available?
-   Are there restrictions on commercial activity?
-   What are the current monthly utility costs?
-   Is the property owner-responsible or tenant-responsible for utilities?
-   Is there an existing reserve study?
-   Are there unused rooms or common areas?
-   Does the property have adequate electrical capacity for EV chargers?
-   Are there current vendor contracts that can be reviewed?

The AI should update its recommendations as new information is added.



AI Scenario Modeling

Allow users to model possible outcomes.

Examples:

-   What happens if the association rents 20 storage lockers?
-   How much could EV charging produce annually?
-   How much could energy improvements reduce monthly expenses?
-   How would $30,000 in new annual income affect condo fees?
-   How much additional reserve funding could be created?
-   What is the estimated payback period for a laundry upgrade?
-   Which three opportunities produce the highest estimated return?

Display conservative, moderate, and optimistic projections.



Human Review and Approval

The AI must not automatically implement financial, legal, lease, vendor, or property changes.

Authorized users must review and approve all recommendations.

For condo associations, allow recommendations to be:

-   Saved for board review
-   Added to a meeting agenda
-   Shared with board members
-   Included in a proposal
-   Added to a vote
-   Assigned for further research
-   Marked as approved, declined, or under consideration



Alerts and Continuous Monitoring

The AI should continuously review new property information and notify authorized users when:

-   A new revenue opportunity is identified

-   A vendor contract may be approaching renewal
-   A revenue-producing asset is underperforming
-   A suggested opportunity becomes more practical because of new property data
-   A completed improvement is not producing expected results

AI Safety and Accuracy

The AI must:

-   Clearly identify assumptions
-   Show what property information was used
-   Identify missing information
-   Avoid guaranteed income claims
-   Avoid making final legal, tax, zoning, insurance, or investment decisions
-   Recommend verification with qualified professionals
-   Refer users to official sources when permits, laws, rebates, or regulations are involved
-   Avoid recommending activities prohibited by leases, governing documents, zoning, or local rules
-   Maintain an audit history showing when recommendations were generated and what data supported them

The goal is to create an AI that understands each property, analyzes its actual assets and financial information, and produces useful revenue and cost-reduction strategies tailored to landlords, commercial property owners, property managers, and condominium associations.
